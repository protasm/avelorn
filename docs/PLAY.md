# Getting started in Avelorn

Open Avelorn's direct player endpoint in a Telnet or GMCP-capable MUD client. If you're on the JVMud directory, select Avelorn to get that endpoint. Follow the host's TLS or tunnel instructions for password accounts.

Choose an account ID and password, then name your Human adventurer. Choose male or female and one of Fighter, Ranger, Mage, or Cleric. Any class can complete the story solo.

## Your first job

You start at the Westwatch Guest-House. Try `look`, `details`, and `exa lamp`. Iona Venn has a job for you. Type `work` to take it and `journal` to read the instructions. Take the coastal path to the signal, `consider wolf`, then `attack wolf`. Combat continues on its own while you decide when to use a technique or brace. Return to Iona and type `report`.

`hints on` marks words you can examine. `hints off` returns to plain prose. `brief` makes movement descriptions shorter and still lists inspectable scenery.

## Useful commands

| Command | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `abilities`, `ability <technique> [target]`, `style` | See your class's techniques and choose a fighting style. |
| `inventory`, `equipment`, `equip <item>` | See what you carry and change your gear. |
| `list`, `buy ration`, `sell <item>` | Trade at a market. |
| `south`, `enter`, `out`, `walk east north` | Travel along local exits or queue a short walking route. |
| `queue`, `stop`, `flee <exit>` | See your travel reserve, clear queued input, or break away from a fight. |
| `rest`, `camp` | Recover at a hearth or spend a ration to recover in the wilderness. |
| `routes`, `board Havenrode`, `trails` | Find staffed departures or a marked walking route. |
| `notices`, `work <id>`, `report <id>` | Choose and finish a noticeboard job. |
| `who`, `tell <name> <message>`, `party` | Find companions and see party commands. |
| `save`, `quit` | Save your character and take a break. |

The ferry at Westwatch is free. Follow the campaign through the five nations, or take a local job and return later. At level 10, the capital notices include weekly expeditions that award regional keepsakes and equipment.
